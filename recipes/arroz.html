Arroz Amarillo

Arroz Amarillo

A staple in a lot of Puerto Rican dishes and one of the most flavorful yet simple rice dishes you'll have!

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons achiote oil or olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons sofrito
  • 1 teaspoon sazon seasoning
  • 1 cube or 1 teaspoon chicken bouillon
  • 2 cups white rice
  • 2 cups water

Steps

  1. Warm oil in a medium sized pot over medium heat. Add sofrito, sazon, and bouillon. Mix together, simmer until fragrant or about 2 minutes.
  2. Add the rice and stir to coat with all the seasonings. Toast for 1 minute before adding the water and stirring to combine.
  3. Cover the pot, turn the heat up to high, bring rice to a low boil. Continue to simmer while covered for 3 minutes, then turn heat to low and cook for about 15-20 minutes or until the rice is cooked to your liking.
  4. Fluff up the rice with a spoon or fork and then serve.
