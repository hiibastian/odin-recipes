Tostones

Fried plaintain fritters! A great snack, appetizer, and your new obsession!

Ingredients

  • 4 green plaintains
  • 1 cup vegetable or canola oil
  • 4 cloves minced garlic
  • 1 tablespoon kosher salt, plus more to garnish
  • 1 lime
  • Mojo verde, mayo-ketchup, or other sauce of choice

Steps

  1. Cut the ends of the plantains and then skin it like you would a potato.
  2. Use your fingers to pry off the tough outer skin and discard.
  3. Cut the plantains into pieces about 1" thick.
  4. Fill a large, deep skillet with oil enough to almost submerge the plantains. Heat over medium for a few minutes to warm oil slightly.
  5. Combine water, garlic, salt, and lime juice. Set aside.
  6. Make sure the oil is just warm enough to lightly bubble. Add the plantain slices. Fry the slices until softened and gold all over, about 4 minutes per side. Then transfer to a paper towel lined plate.
  7. Gently flatten the plantains with the bottom of a glass or plate, just enough they flatten out but not too thin to break.
  8. Dip the flattened plantains into the garlic-lime water. Let it sit for a few seconds, then remove from water and pat dry. Repeat with all plantains. Sprinkle with salt. Serve with sauce of choice.
